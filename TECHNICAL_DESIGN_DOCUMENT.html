Diagnostic Lab Automated Report-Delivery Portal for Patients and Referring Doctors

1. Executive Summary & Problem Context

Company A operates a diagnostic chain with 6 central labs and 38 outpatient collection centres across Vijayawada and coastal Andhra Pradesh. Their existing workflow involved 260 staff using 9 shared phones to manually dispatch unencrypted signed PDF reports over WhatsApp and SMS.

This process incurred significant operational friction and legal risks under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023: reports remained permanently in chats, 15 reports a month were misdirected to wrong numbers, patients overwhelmed front-desks with status inquiries (40 staff hours/day), and critical panic results (e.g. Potassium 6.8 mmol/L) went unintercepted.

ArogyaLink introduces a zero-install, secure web portal governed by 72-hour signed expiring links, mobile OTP authentication, deterministic clinical AI summaries in 26+ Indian languages (including Telugu, Tenglish, and Hinglish), and an automated panic-value emergency intercept.

2. Labeled Assumptions (Directly from Client Brief)

  • [ASSUMPTION-01] Patient Volume: 2,000 patients/day over 360 operational days/year = 7,20,000 diagnostic orders annually.
  • [ASSUMPTION-02] Front-Desk Inefficiency: 30% of patients call or visit about status (600 visits/day × 4 mins = 40 staff hours/day × 360 days = 14,400 hours × ₹250 = ₹36 Lakh/year).
  • [ASSUMPTION-03] Paper Waste: 40% of reports printed and hand-delivered (2,88,000 reports × ₹12 = ₹34.56 Lakh). Total operational waste equals ₹70.56 Lakh annually.
  • [ASSUMPTION-04] Family Phone Sharing: One mobile number frequently covers multi-generational families in semi-urban and rural Andhra Pradesh.
  • [ASSUMPTION-05] Statutory Retention Windows: 72 hours for patient expiring links under DPDP data minimization; 90 days active consultation window for referring clinicians.
  • [ASSUMPTION-06] Budget & Delivery Milestone: ₹6.5 Lakh budget across 6 weeks targeting an 80% digital adoption rate and 60% call reduction within 3 months.

4. Core Data Models

The system defines clean, structured interfaces for Orders, Users, Audit Logs, and Callback Tasks:

// Diagnostic Order Schema
interface DiagnosticOrder {
  orderId: string;              // e.g. "ORD-71204"
  patientName: string;          // e.g. "Lakshmi Prasanna K"
  age: number; gender: string;
  patientPhone: string;         // e.g. "+91 98765 43210"
  testName: string; parameter: string;
  result: string | number; unit: string;
  referenceRange: string; status: 'Ready' | 'Processing' | 'Critical';
  isCritical: boolean; referringDoctor: string;
  collectionCentre: string; createdAt: string;
}

5. Security, Cryptography & DPDP Act 2023 Compliance

  • 72-Hour Signed Expiring Links: Generated via HMAC-SHA256 signatures binding Order ID with an expiry timestamp. Tampering with parameters invalidates the cryptographic hash immediately.
  • 2-Factor Mobile OTP Gate: Entering the URL presents a 4-digit OTP verification gate sent to the registered mobile number.
  • Phone Number Masking: Personal identifiers are automatically masked across all screens and audit logs (e.g. +91 98765 *****).
  • Immutable Audit Logging: Every decryption, OTP challenge, and dispatch event is logged with timestamps and masked credentials for statutory regulatory inspection.

6. Clinical AI Mechanism & Patient Safety Safeguards

🚨 The #1 Safety Rule (Critical Potassium Intercept):
For life-threatening results—specifically serum Potassium ≥ 6.0 mmol/L (Farzana Begum, 6.8 mmol/L):
  • Automated AI summaries are strictly WITHHELD and BLOCKED.
  • An unmissable high-contrast Red Emergency Banner appears directing the patient to hospital care.
  • The server automatically creates an Urgent Doctor Callback Task on the front-desk console requiring staff to confirm direct contact with the referring physician.

Multilingual Translation: Synthesizes plain-language summaries across 26+ Indian languages, with full support for Telugu (తెలుగు), Tenglish, Hinglish, and Hindi, backed by an in-app doubt chat assistant and a closed-loop Pathologist Audit Queue (50 summaries/week).

7. UI/UX Decisions & Clinical Accessibility

  • Medical Aurora Ambient Background: Soft floating emerald and teal orbs with a geometric dot matrix pattern replace sterile white backgrounds.
  • Glassmorphic Elevated Cards: High-contrast readable typography optimized for low-end mobile screens and elderly patients.
  • Search-Driven Front-Desk: Records are dynamically revealed through intelligent searching by name, mobile, order ID, or parameter, sorted by clinical priority triage (Critical panic values first).
  • Accessible Printable Report: Formatted to match standard pathology paper with biological reference ranges, doctor signatures, and a dedicated @media print PDF stylesheet.
